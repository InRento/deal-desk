Deal Desk – InRento's underwriting app (test version)

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and Google Drive for Desktop, signed in with your InRento account,
with the "Deal desk" shared drive visible in Finder (Google Drive > Shared drives > Deal desk).

1. Unzip, then drag "Deal Desk.app" to your Applications folder (or the Desktop).
2. Double-click it. macOS will say it cannot check the app because it is not from the App Store.
   Click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Deal Desk,
   then confirm with your password. (Older macOS: right-click the app > Open > Open.)
   This is needed once.
3. The app opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks.

Deals are kept in the "Deal desk" shared drive, so everyone on the team sees the same deals. Google Drive for Desktop
does the syncing; the app itself only reads and writes files in that folder. Your Mac keeps a local index, your Gmail
cache and a log in the folder "Deal Desk" in your home folder.

Gmail (optional): Home page > Email settings, with a Gmail app password. Read-only – the app never changes your mailbox,
and the password is kept in the macOS Keychain.

To close it, just close the browser tab; it stops by itself 20 minutes later.
To remove it: delete the app and the "Deal Desk" folder in your home folder (the deals stay on the shared drive).
