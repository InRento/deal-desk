Deal Desk – InRento's underwriting app

You need: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and Google Drive for Desktop, signed in with your InRento account,
with the "Deal desk" shared drive showing in Finder (Google Drive > Shared drives > Deal desk).
Tip: right-click the "Deal desk" shared drive > Offline access > Available offline, so Deal Desk starts without waiting
for downloads.

1. Drag "Deal Desk" onto the Applications folder next to it. Then eject this disk image.
2. Open Deal Desk from Applications. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app: click Done, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Deal Desk and confirm with your
   password. (Older macOS: right-click the app > Open > Open.) Needed once.
3. The first start fetches Deal Desk from the shared drive (a few seconds) and opens it in your browser at
   http://127.0.0.1:8765. Keep it in the Dock.

Updates come by themselves: when a new version is published on the shared drive, Deal Desk shows
"Restart to update". There is nothing to reinstall.

Deals live in the "Deal desk" shared drive, so everyone on the team sees the same deals. To change a deal, press
"Lock & edit"; others see it read-only until you unlock it. Your Mac keeps only a local index, your Gmail cache and a
log in the folder "Deal Desk" in your home folder.

Gmail (optional): Home page > Email settings, with a Gmail app password. Read-only – the app never changes your mailbox,
and the password is kept in the macOS Keychain.

To close it, just close the browser tab; it stops by itself 20 minutes later.
To remove it: delete the app and the "Deal Desk" folder in your home folder (the deals stay on the shared drive).
